# Communication style cheatsheets

Free Markdown cheatsheets and a JSON/YAML dataset for four everyday communication habits — **Clarity, Connection, Structure, and Spark** — plus the five result styles from the [Communication Style Quiz](https://commstylequiz.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=a_repo&utm_content=readme).

This is a vocabulary for what someone seems to want from a conversation right now: a decision, a listen, a plan, or a new idea. It is **not** a personality test, not a diagnosis, and not a measure of how good you are with people.

**[Take the free 13-question quiz — about 3 minutes, no sign-up](https://commstylequiz.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=a_repo&utm_content=readme)**

Your answers are scored in your browser and are not sent to the site.

## The four habits, in 30 seconds

| Habit | Style name | One-liner | How to talk to this person |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clarity | The Pathfinder | Says it straight, gets it done. | Lead with the point. Tell me what you need and by when. |
| Connection | The Bridge-Builder | Hears what people don't say. | Check in with me as a person first. Tell me how you really feel about it. |
| Structure | The Blueprint | Has a plan for the plan. | Give me the details and a little time. Specific examples help more than general feelings. |
| Spark | The Spark | Turns any chat into an idea. | Talk it through with me live. Build on my idea before you poke holes in it. |

If your answers spread fairly evenly, the quiz result is **The Navigator**: “Speaks every style fluently.” Tell that person what the conversation is for: a decision, a listen, a plan or ideas.

People switch. Someone who wants connection at home may want clarity at work. Under stress, many of us lean harder on one habit. The useful move is not to typecast a partner or a colleague. It is to ask, in ordinary language: “Do you want a decision, a listen, a plan, or ideas?”

## How the quiz scoring works

These are the public rules, also in [data/styles.json](data/styles.json):

1. Each of the 13 answers adds one point to one habit. You can only pick one option per question, so the result shows what you prefer when you have to choose — not how good you are at any of them.
2. If the highest score minus the lowest score is 2 or less, the result is The Navigator (balanced).
3. Otherwise the main style is the habit with the most points. Ties are shown openly, in a fixed order (Clarity, Connection, Structure, Spark). If your second style is only one point behind, the result page says so.

Thirteen questions is a snapshot. Treat the result as a default, not a fixed label.

## What this is not

The quiz and these cheatsheets are for adults (18+), for fun and self-reflection only. They are not a psychological, clinical or diagnostic test. They are not scientifically validated. They do not measure personality, mental health, intelligence, or how “good” you are with people. They must not be used to predict whether a relationship will work, or to make hiring or career decisions.

If two people get different results, that is ordinary. It does not mean you are incompatible.

The model is original. It is not derived from MBTI, DISC, SOCIAL STYLE, or any commercial instrument, and this project is not affiliated with those publishers.

If you are struggling, a quiz is not a substitute for talking to a qualified professional.
